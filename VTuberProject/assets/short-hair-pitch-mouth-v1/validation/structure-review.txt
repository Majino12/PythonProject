Three fixed-pose mouth registration audit — 2026-10-26

Scope: view-left-up, view-right-up, view-left-down; twelve new native 1122×1402 mouth paintings. This audit proposes only local translation-only patches. It does not approve the paintings on behalf of the user, and does not change the previously approved face, eye, jaw-opening, gaze or brow registration.

Anatomical upper-lip references remain [405,574], [690,588], [454,711]. These are not crop centers. The three generated pucker central creases are offset within their otherwise fixed portraits, requiring once-only source registration: left-up [385,572]→[405,574], right-up [710,584]→[690,588], left-down [434,715]→[454,711]. Those source paintings contain genuinely narrower pursed creases; a neutral smile is not being translated to manufacture a pucker. Their native scale stays 1:1. The left-up and left-down pucker clip polygons are inset on the jaw-facing side so the translated source jawline cannot enter the patch.

All six directional paintings keep their source x coordinates. Their visible crease centers independently establish the direction: left-up +7 to +7.5 / −4 pixels; right-up final shiftLeft +5 to +6 / shiftRight −7 to −8 pixels; left-down +5.5 to +6.5 / −2 to −3 pixels. Differences reflect antialias thresholds, not two registrations. The patch anchor uses the original anatomical reference plus the selected measured displacement. Only a fixed y correction puts the central seam back at the approved upper-lip height. Character-left means canvas-right; no mirroring is used.

Rejected candidates matter: the first left-up press was nearly the neutral smile and the first right-up shiftLeft only lifted its corner without a positive center shift. They are not used by the proposed registration. The left-up press-v2 visibly flattens/tucks the right corner, though its compression is restrained (approximately 76-pixel crease versus the original 84-pixel crease). Right-up press is more visibly downturned; left-down press is a shorter flatter closed line. These are discrete maximum-state drawings, not a full strength sequence.

Left-up pucker-v3 retains faint bright lower-lip flecks. Compared with v1, one bright point is weaker, but v3 still has a local bright pixel around source [390,582], RGB [253,238,232], against green-channel values around 210–220 nearby. It must not be described as fully matte or as successful complete gloss removal. The visible line is thin and closed, without a thick painted lip, but the remaining point must be judged in the actual composite.

Source measurement was read-only with PIL. All PNGs are untouched by this audit. Pixel measurements include two darkness thresholds and SHA-256 provenance so a changed final source cannot silently inherit these coordinates. Actual browser composites and mouth close-ups are still required to judge residual smile lines, registration seams and the remaining lip highlights; geometry and protected-region checks alone do not establish visual quality.
