Pure left/right mouth direction repair

Scope: only the new work/VTuberProject copy. No original project, former v0.13 experiment, assets, GitHub, or accepted concept image was modified.

Behavior
- Removed the unsigned legacy `side` selection. Its legacy atlas alias can remain as unused data, but it is no longer a required frame or an output.
- Optional manifest.sequences.mouthLeft / mouthRight opt in independently. Each contains separate non-neutral painting keys in weak-to-strong order and uses anatomical character direction.
- Signed mouthLeft minus mouthRight chooses the correct independent sequence. Equal coefficients cancel, zero coefficients release, and each direction has its own hysteresis.
- Priority: press > closure > directional/center pucker > funnel > supported pure shift > emotion > jaw.
- Without a pure-shift sequence, pure direction input preserves the other current mouth coefficients, and the capability is `unsupported`. It never falls back to a directional pucker or `side`.
- Pucker + left/right remains independently supported by its existing pucker sequences and retains correct sign.
- Support becomes `frame` only if the sequence exists with its own keys and distinct source rectangles in every registered mouth atlas/pose. Validation rejects missing frames or aliases to another mouth shape/direction. A malformed replacement pack does not silently advertise a missing capability.
- Pure mouthLeft / mouthRight support remains `unsupported` for the actual old Luna v0.13 pack, which still has no dedicated pure-shift artwork.

Gallery

Limits
- This fixes signal/asset correctness, not visual quality. No new mouth artwork was generated; the old pack still cannot display pure mouth shifts.
- Distinct source rectangles prevent known manifest aliases but cannot prove the pixel artwork or anatomy is correct. Visual acceptance is still required.
- Other inherited integration tests still snapshot old eye and mouth positions; those assertions must not be cited as evidence that their perspective or placement is visually correct.
